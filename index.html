Storage & privacy

ClassVision stores account, student and attendance data in IndexedDB in this browser. Data is not automatically synced to another phone or computer.

Student recordsOn this device
Camera framesProcessed in browser
Face templatesStored locally in browser

Workspace tools

Important limitation

This is a client-side prototype for supervised classroom testing, not a secure institutional attendance platform. Browser storage can be cleared, local passwords do not provide server-grade account security, and records do not sync across devices. Always verify identity yourself before confirming attendance.
